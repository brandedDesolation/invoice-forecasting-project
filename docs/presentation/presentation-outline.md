# Presentation Outline

## Slide 1: Title
**VICAI: AI-Assisted Invoice Operations Platform**

Message:
- We built a finance operations platform that combines OCR, review, workflow, payments, and analytics.

## Slide 2: Problem
Message:
- Traditional invoice handling is fragmented.
- Finance teams lose time on manual entry, review, follow-up, and reporting.
- OCR alone is not enough because extracted data still needs operational control.

Talking points:
- manual extraction risk
- missing workflow visibility
- weak supplier visibility
- poor correction learning

## Slide 3: Solution
Message:
- VICAI closes the operational loop from invoice ingestion to workflow action and analytics.

Core loop:
- upload
- extract
- review
- approve
- remind
- analyze

## Slide 4: Product Scope
Message:
- The product now includes:
  - secure admin login
  - invoice upload with PDF/image OCR
  - review queue
  - invoice detail operations
  - supplier management
  - workflow approvals and reminders
  - payments
  - analytics and learning loop
